## The Obelisk Gate
### N. K. Jemison, 2016
Well I enjoyed *The Fifth Season*, despite having misgivings about the seemingly gratuitous violence and gore, so I went ahead and started on *The Obelisk Gate* but I couldn't keep going to the finish. Somewhere around page 355 (out of 407) I decided I'd had enough, that the author was in love with inventing novel but uniformly grotesque ways for characters to kill each other, and that a lot of the plot and characterization was just vehicle for the spectacle of terror. Hugo winner or not, I'm not interested.  TBH in the 87 percent of the book I read, I'm not sure what the Hugo was for. The world had been mostly presented in *The Fifth Season*, which yes I see a Hugo there easily, but I had way less sense of wonderment with #2.   There's a third book as well.
